Last updated: September 8, 2026

ytaiagent is an AI-assisted tool designed to help users create and manage YouTube video content and perform YouTube-related tasks authorized by the user.

2. Information We Access

When you connect your Google or YouTube account to ytaiagent, the application may access Google and YouTube information permitted by the permissions that you explicitly authorize.

The application accesses only the information necessary to provide the features that you have requested and authorized.

3. How We Use Information

Information obtained through Google and YouTube services is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, including authorized YouTube content creation and management functions.

We do not sell Google user data or use Google user data for advertising.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information or Google user data. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide a user-requested service, when authorized by the user, or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality or as required by applicable law.

Users may stop the application's access to their Google or YouTube account by revoking the application's access through their Google Account settings.

7. Revoking Access

You can revoke ytaiagent's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account security settings.

8. Children's Privacy

ytaiagent is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or ytaiagent, please contact the developer using the developer contact information associated with this application.
